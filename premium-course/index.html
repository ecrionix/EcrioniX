❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EcrioniX free courses.

Are all courses on EcrioniX completely free?

Yes. Every single course, lesson, code example, and interactive lab on EcrioniX is 100% free and open to everyone worldwide. There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no paywalls.

Do I need to install commercial EDA tools?

No. All code examples can be run directly using our built-in online Verilog simulator and synthesis viewers. For advanced ASIC physical design sign-off, industry-standard tool commands (Synopsys, Cadence, OpenROAD) are documented for reference.

Do I need an account to read the lessons?

No. All courses and lessons are freely readable without creating an account or signing in. An optional free account is available if you want to track completed problems.

Are the courses self-paced?

Yes. All courses on EcrioniX are 100% self-paced. You can start, pause, and review technical modules whenever your schedule permits.

Are certificates available upon course completion?

Formal verifiable completion certificates are currently in active development. When launched, completed problem katas and curriculum modules will count toward certificate eligibility.

How do I report a technical error in a lesson?

You can reach out directly via the Community discussion forum or contact the engineering team via our contact form. We appreciate technical feedback from peer engineers.
