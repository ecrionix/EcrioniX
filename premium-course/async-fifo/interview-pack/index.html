Lesson 5.2

Interview pack — whiteboard answers

Say these out loud until they are automatic. Each answer is the version that survives a follow-up “are you sure?”

1

Rapid fire

QuestionModel answer
Why a FIFO?Elastic buffer for rate matching and backpressure. It does not create bandwidth.
Overflow vs stall?Overflow = write still commits when occupancy is DEPTH (data lost). Stall = wr_en high, full high, wr_fire=0. Same idea for underflow vs empty.
How do you prove none?Golden queue of DEPTH + SVA: never wr_fire at occ==DEPTH, never rd_fire at occ==0.
Sync vs async?One clock vs two. Async: dual-port RAM, Gray pointers, 2FF, full in wr domain, empty in rd domain.
Why not 2FF a data bus?Reconvergent multi-bit CDC — bits sampled at different times invent illegal vectors.
Why Gray for pointers?Adjacent counts differ by one bit. Synchronizer captures old or new, both legal.
Why not Gray the data?Payload is not a unit-distance counter. Keep data in RAM; only pointers cross.
Empty formula?In rd domain: (next) read Gray equals 2FF-synced write Gray.
Full formula?In wr domain: (next) write Gray equals synced read Gray with two MSBs inverted.
Binary full vs Gray full?Binary extra-MSB inverts one bit. Gray inverts two MSBs. Do not mix.
XOR then flop or flop then XOR?Convert bin_next to gray_next, then register. CDC the flop output.
DEPTH?Power of two for this Gray style. Size from worst-case burst plus CDC pointer latency.
Reset?Clear both pointer engines and both 2FF chains. Sync deassert per domain.
Why conservative flags?2FF delay hides recent opposite-side traffic. Prefer false full/empty over overflow/underflow.
2

Whiteboard — 8 minutes

  1. Draw dual-port RAM, wr_clk box, rd_clk box.
  2. wbin, wgray, 2FF of rgray, full compare.
  3. rbin, rgray, 2FF of wgray, empty compare.
  4. Write bin2gray = x^(x>>1) and the two flag equations.
  5. State: overflow is a design bug; the producer must honor full.

Coding drill

In the simulator, implement only bin2gray and the full comparator for PTR_W=4. Stimulus: walk wbin 0..16 with rbin=0; full must rise exactly when occupancy hits 8 for DEPTH=8 (PTR_W=4, 2^3=8).

2-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute scripts

2 min: “Async FIFO is a dual-clock queue. Data sits in dual-port RAM. I Gray-code extra-wide pointers, 2FF them, generate full where I write and empty where I read.”

5 min: Add binary CDC failure (7→8), Gray unit distance, conservative flags, power-of-two depth.

15 min: Derive empty and two-MSB full, reset, almost-full via gray2bin, SVA $onehot0(gray ^ $past(gray)), and why registered Gray matters.
