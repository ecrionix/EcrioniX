=== FILE: pipeline-throughput/index.html ===
K=4 ops, N=3 stages, T=1 cycle each:

Non-pipelined:  |--op0(3)--|--op1(3)--|--op2(3)--|--op3(3)--|  → 12 cycles
Pipelined:      |fill|s0|s1|s2|s0|s1|s2|s0|s1|s2|s0|s1|s2|     → 6 cycles
                 ↑ 3-cycle latency per op, but 1 op/cycle after fill
=== END ===

=== FILE: skid-buffer/index.html ===
Scenario: downstream stalls 1 cycle while upstream sends 2 beats
clk        _/‾\_/‾\_/‾\_/‾\_/‾\_/‾\_/‾\_/‾\_
s_valid    _______/‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾\____________
s_data     ------&lt; A &gt;--&lt; B &gt;--------------
s_ready    ‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾\_______/‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾
m_valid    _____________/‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾\______
m_data     -------------&lt; A &gt;--&lt; B &gt;--------
m_ready    ‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾\___________/‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾

Cycle 4: m_ready=0 but s_ready=1 — beat B enters skid while A waits on m_data
Cycle 5: m_ready=1 — A transfers; skid shifts B into main on next edge
=== END ===
