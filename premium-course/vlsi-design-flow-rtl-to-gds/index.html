Excerpt: The 12 Classical Stages of Modern ASIC Flow

From architectural specification to tapeout, modern digital integrated circuits follow an automated, constraint-driven implementation flow:

1. Architecture & Specification -> System microarchitecture definition
2. RTL Design & Linting       -> Synthesizable Verilog / SystemVerilog
3. Functional Verification    -> Constrained-random simulation & assertions
4. Logic Synthesis            -> Tech-independent Boolean optimization (Yosys)
5. Technology Cell Mapping    -> Translation into standard-cells (.lib)
6. Design for Test (DFT)      -> Scan chain insertion & ATPG fault coverage
7. Floorplanning & PDN        -> Core aspect ratio, macro placement & power straps
8. Standard Cell Placement    -> Global & detailed placement, wirelength minimization
9. Clock Tree Synthesis (CTS) -> Skew-balanced clock distribution network (H-Tree)
10. Global & Detailed Routing -> Metal layer interconnect routing (FastRoute/TritonRoute)
11. Extraction & STA Signoff  -> RC parasitics extraction (SPEF) & timing closure
12. Physical Signoff & GDSII  -> DRC / LVS checks & foundry tapeout export
