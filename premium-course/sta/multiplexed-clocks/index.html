Lesson 4.8

Multiplexed Clocks

When a design can select between multiple clocks, STA needs to analyze paths for all active modes.

1

The Challenge

A clock MUX propagates two different clocks. By default, STA tries to analyze cross-domain timing between these clocks, assuming they could be active simultaneously.

2

Exclusive Clock Groups

We must tell the tool that the two clocks entering the MUX are physically exclusive. We use:

set_clock_groups -physically_exclusive -group clk_fast -group clk_slow

Warning: Ensure glitch-free clock muxes in RTL, otherwise switching clocks on the fly will corrupt state.
