Clock Tree Synthesis (CTS) Basics

CTS is the process of building the buffer tree that distributes the clock to thousands of flip-flops.

1

Goals of CTS

  • Minimize Skew: Ensure all flip-flops receive the clock edge at precisely the same time.
  • Minimize Insertion Delay: Keep the total latency low to reduce OCV (On-Chip Variation) impacts.
  • Minimize Power: Use clock gating and optimal buffer sizing.
2

Topologies

Common structures include H-trees, star routing, and mesh networks. H-trees perfectly balance wire lengths to theoretically achieve zero skew, although process variations mean true zero skew is impossible.

Useful Skew

Modern tools intentionally introduce non-zero skew (useful skew) to borrow time from faster paths to help meet setup on slower paths.
