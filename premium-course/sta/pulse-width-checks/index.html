Lesson 4.9

Pulse Width Checks

Standard cells require a minimum high and low pulse width to function correctly.

1

Degradation

As a clock propagates through unequal rise and fall times in buffers, its duty cycle can distort. A 50% duty cycle clock might shrink to a 20% pulse width.

2

Minimum Pulse Width

If the pulse becomes too narrow, a flip-flop might not register the edge, leading to catastrophic failure. STA enforces min_pulse_width checks across all clock pins.
