Lesson 8.4

Multicycle Paths (Setup)

A set_multicycle_path exception informs the STA tool that the data from the launch flop does not need to be captured exactly on the very next clock edge, but rather N cycles later.

1

Why Multicycle?

Suppose you have a complex combinational multiplier that takes almost two full clock cycles to settle. If you try to force STA to meet a single-cycle setup time, the synthesis tool will fail, insert massive buffers, or scale up cell sizes, burning immense power. By designing the RTL with an enable signal that only samples the multiplier result every two cycles, you can safely declare an MCP of 2.

MCP Setup Syntax
set_multicycle_path -setup 2 -from [get_cells launch_reg] -to [get_cells capture_reg]

This command shifts the setup check forward by 1 cycle (so it is checked 2 cycles after launch instead of 1).
