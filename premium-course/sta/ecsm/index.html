Lesson 6.4

Effective Current Source Model (ECSM)

While Synopsys pushed CCS, Cadence introduced a competing standard: the Effective Current Source Model (ECSM).

1

What is ECSM?

Instead of modeling the output driver as a direct current source (like CCS), ECSM models the delay and output voltage waveform by directly capturing the output voltage curve over time for different loads and slews.

ECSM Mechanism

It characterizes the output voltage at multiple trip points (e.g., 10%, 20%, 50%, 80%, 90% of VDD) rather than just a single slew threshold. The STA tool then reverse-engineers the effective driver current required to produce that exact voltage waveform.

2

CCS vs ECSM

Both models solve the same problem: the NLDM inaccuracy in deep sub-micron due to high interconnect resistance and Miller capacitance.

  • CCS stores Current over time. (Driven by Synopsys Liberty format).
  • ECSM stores Voltage over time. (Driven by Cadence/Liberty extensions).

Industry Reality: Today, the `.lib` format supports both, and modern STA tools (PrimeTime, Tempus) are perfectly capable of reading and interpolating both CCS and ECSM accurately. They offer comparable SPICE-level accuracy (typically within 2% of SPICE).
