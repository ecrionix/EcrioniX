Lesson 4.5

Clock Uncertainty

A margin of error subtracted from the clock period to account for unmodeled variations.

1

Pre-CTS vs Post-CTS

Before CTS, uncertainty models both estimated skew and jitter. After CTS, skew is accurately calculated by the propagated clocks, so uncertainty is reduced to model only jitter and margin.

  • Pre-CTS Uncertainty: Jitter + Estimated Skew + Design Margin
  • Post-CTS Uncertainty: Jitter + Design Margin
2

Setup and Hold

Uncertainty tightens both setup and hold requirements.

Hold Timing Warning: Setting excessive hold uncertainty post-CTS can lead the tool to over-buffer datapaths, burning power and area for phantom violations.
