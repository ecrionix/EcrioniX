STA / Module 8: Timing Exceptions
Lesson 8.7

Disable Timing (set_disable_timing)

The set_disable_timing constraint is a drastic measure. It tells the tool to break a timing arc completely within a specific library cell.

1

Breaking Combinational Loops

STA works on directed acyclic graphs (DAGs). If your RTL contains a combinational loop (e.g., a ring oscillator or an accidental transparent latch structure), the STA engine cannot resolve it and will often issue an error or aggressively break the loop at an arbitrary point.

To safely manage loops, or to prevent timing through a specific arc in a complex IP (like an analog block model), you explicitly sever the connection.

Breaking an arc
# Breaks the arc from input 'A' to output 'Y' on instance 'u_osc_buf'
set_disable_timing -from A -to Y [get_cells u_osc_buf]

Unlike set_false_path (which ignores timing between a source and a destination flop), set_disable_timing severs the structural graph at a specific gate.
