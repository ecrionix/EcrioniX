Lesson 9.9

VT Swapping (LVT, RVT, HVT)

Sometimes you cannot make a cell physically larger due to density constraints. Instead, we change the Threshold Voltage (VT) of the cell to make it faster or slower.

1

The Multi-VT Strategy

Modern standard cell libraries offer the exact same logic gate and footprint (size) in multiple flavors based on the threshold voltage of the transistors:

FlavorSpeedLeakage PowerUse Case
LVT (Low VT)FastestExtremely HighFixing critical setup violations
RVT/SVT (Regular/Standard VT)MediumMediumBulk of the design logic
HVT (High VT)SlowestVery LowFixing hold violations & power saving
2

ECO VT Swapping

Because swapping an RVT cell to an LVT cell doesn't change the physical footprint, it is a highly preferred method during final routing stages to fix setup, as it does not disrupt neighboring cells.

Power Penalty: LVT cells leak significantly more current even when idle. Swapping too many cells to LVT will blow up your static power budget. The goal is to use LVT only on the absolute most critical paths.
