Lesson 1.5

Hold Time at the Transistor Level

Hold time exists because the transmission gate inside the flop does not close instantly. The input must remain stable while the door is shutting.

1

The Shutting Door

At the rising edge of the clock, the Master latch transitions from transparent to opaque. The transmission gate takes a finite, non-zero amount of time to fully turn off (due to the finite slew rate of the clock and internal RC delays).

Definition

Hold time (Thd) is the minimum time data must remain stable AFTER the active clock edge to ensure the Master latch doesn't overwrite its captured value with new data.

A hold violation means the "new" data managed to sneak into the Master latch before the transmission gate completely closed, corrupting the stored state.
