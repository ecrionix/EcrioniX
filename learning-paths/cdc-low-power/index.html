Recommended Learning Methodology

  • Step 1 — Understand the Concept: Read each module's core objectives and referenced EcrioniX guide.
  • Step 2 — Code the RTL: Open each practice challenge and implement top_module matching the exact ports and timing contract.
  • Step 3 — Inspect Waveforms: Run the remote cloud simulator to generate timing waveforms (VCD). Compare your signal transitions with the golden WaveDrom contract.
  • Step 4 — Review Explanations: Even after passing, review the editorial notes and hardware considerations to understand ASIC synthesis tradeoffs.
