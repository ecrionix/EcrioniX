Community Contributor Program

Help build the world's most rigorous, open semiconductor education destination. Submit synthesizable Verilog modules, UVM verification components, and technical timing deep-dives.

The 6-Stage Technical Review Workflow

STAGE 01

Submission

Submit your proposed tutorial, synthesizable module, or testbench via web portal or GitHub Pull Request.

STAGE 02

Technical Review

EDA and RTL peer reviewers verify timing, synthesizability in Yosys/OpenROAD, and adherence to standards.

STAGE 03

Corner-Case Hardening

Reviewers work with you to test edge cases: reset hazards, metastability windows, and throughput bottlenecks.

STAGE 04

Peer Approval

Formal signoff from at least one verified industry engineer or researcher before publishing.

STAGE 05

Official Publication

Your module is published with full interactive browser simulation permalinks on EcrioniX.

STAGE 06

Attribution & Badge

Prominently featured author attribution, LinkedIn/GitHub links, and permanent Contributor Hall of Fame listing.

📋 Editorial Standards & Original-Work Policy

  • ✓
    Zero AI Hallucinations Policy: Every mathematical derivation, SDC syntax, and standard cell attribute must be grounded in verified silicon practice or official IEEE 1364/1800/ARM AMBA standards.
  • ✓
    Strict Synthesizability: All HDL submissions must synthesize clean in Yosys without unintended latches, combinational feedback loops, or multi-driven nets.
  • ✓
    Self-Checking Testbenches Required: Code submissions must be accompanied by a self-checking testbench that exercises corner cases (full/empty boundaries, reset timing, and back-to-back transfers).
  • ✓
    Originality & Open Licensing: We strictly prohibit plagiarized content from textbooks or proprietary corporate IP. Contributors grant publication rights under standard open educational terms.
