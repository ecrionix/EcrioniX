Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between "Smart Crop" and "Fit (Letterbox)"?
Smart Crop (Fill) scales the image so it completely covers the target aspect ratio and trims the excess edges. Fit (Letterbox) shrinks the image so 100% of it is visible and fills any leftover empty space with black, white, or transparent borders.
Which aspect ratio should I use for YouTube and social media?
Use 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails, video covers, and laptop screens. Use 9:16 for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Use 1:1 or 4:5 for standard Instagram posts and avatars.
