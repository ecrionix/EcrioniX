Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reduce an image to 50KB or 100KB?
Click the 50 KB or 100 KB preset button above. The tool automatically runs a binary search algorithm across quality and resolution quantization to compress the image precisely under your specified byte threshold.
Which format gives the best compression?
WebP typically produces the smallest file size (25–35% smaller than JPEG at identical visual quality). JPEG is universally compatible with every device, email client, and form.
