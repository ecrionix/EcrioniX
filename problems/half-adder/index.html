Architectural Deep Dive & Implementation Walkthrough
Adding two 1-bit values $a + b$ produces a 2-bit result $\{cout, sum\} \in \{0, 1, 2\}$. The least-significant bit (Sum) represents modulo-2 addition ($a \oplus b$), and the most-significant bit (Cout) represents the overflow carry ($a \cdot b$).


      
Common RTL Designer Pitfalls
⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid
['Swapping `sum` and `cout` outputs.', 'Using OR instead of XOR for sum, which fails for $1+1$.']


      
Silicon & Hardware Engineering Notes
🛠️ ASIC vs FPGA Hardware Considerations
A half adder cannot accept a carry-in from a previous stage; chaining multiple bits requires full adders with carry-in support.
