Over-Temperature Throttle Monitor

Sequential Design · sequential, power, monitor

Throttle performance at a warning temperature and shut down entirely at a critical one — with a recovery margin so the chip doesn't oscillate in and out of shutdown right at the edge.

Module Specification
Write a synthesizable Verilog module named top_module matching the ports below exactly.
⚡ Timing & Synthesizability Constraints

Timing Contract: Strict synchronous timing aligned to posedge clk or zero-delay combinational propagation.

RTL Constraints: Synthesizable Verilog-2001. No # delays in synthesizable RTL.

Concept: throttle is a simple combinational compare against the WARN threshold, but shutdown needs hysteresis just like a brown-out detector: it latches at CRIT and only releases once temperature drops all the way down to a separate, lower RECOVER threshold. Releasing shutdown as soon as temperature drops back below CRIT (the same threshold that triggered it) removes that recovery margin — the chip would flip in and out of shutdown right at the CRIT boundary instead of needing a real, meaningful drop in temperature to resume.

Problem Statement & Requirements

      
Throttle performance at a warning temperature and shut down entirely at a critical one — with a recovery margin so the chip doesn't oscillate in and out of shutdown right at the edge.

      
Hardware design teams at leading semiconductor firms evaluate these exact port contracts, race-condition assertions, and corner cases during technical design reviews and ASIC engineer interviews.

      
      
Concept: throttle is a simple combinational compare against the WARN threshold, but shutdown needs hysteresis just like a brown-out detector: it latches at CRIT and only releases once temperature drops all the way down to a separate, lower RECOVER threshold. Releasing shutdown as soon as temperature drops back below CRIT (the same threshold that triggered it) removes that recovery margin — the chip would flip in and out of shutdown right at the CRIT boundary instead of needing a real, meaningful drop in temperature to resume.
