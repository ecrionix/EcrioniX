Architectural Deep Dive & Implementation Walkthrough
Modern FPGA and ASIC block memories (BRAM / SRAM macros) operate synchronously: addresses and write enables are sampled at the clock edge, and data appears at the output after the clock-to-out access time ($t_{co}$). Registering the read address or output enables mapping directly into embedded hardware Block RAM primitives rather than distributed LUT RAM.
