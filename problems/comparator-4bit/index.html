Architectural Deep Dive & Implementation Walkthrough
A magnitude comparator evaluates the numerical order of two binary numbers. Hardware implementations perform virtual subtraction $a - b$: the sign of the difference and the zero detector indicate whether $a > b$, $a == b$, or $a < b$.


      
Common RTL Designer Pitfalls
⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid
['Treating operands as signed when unsigned comparison was intended (or vice-versa with `$signed`).', 'Allowing more than one output to be active simultaneously.']


      
Silicon & Hardware Engineering Notes
🛠️ ASIC vs FPGA Hardware Considerations
Equality (`==`) synthesizes into bitwise XNOR gates followed by a wide AND gate. Magnitude (`>` and `<`) uses borrow-lookahead carry chains similar to subtractors.
