What You Must Design & Verify

      
Reverse the bit order of an 8-bit word so the LSB becomes the MSB and vice versa — pure rewiring, no arithmetic.

      
Engineers encounter 8-Bit Reversal as an essential building block in combinational design. Technical interviewers at top semiconductor companies (including NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Apple, and Intel) evaluate these exact port contracts, reset behaviors, and corner-case assertions.

      
      
Concept: Bit reversal is a straight cross-wiring job: out = {in[0],in[1],...,in[7]} maps every bit to its mirror position. Confusing this with a byte/nibble swap ({in[3:0],in[7:4]}) reorders groups of bits but leaves each 4-bit half internally unreversed — a very different, and very common, mistake.
