Architectural Deep Dive & Implementation Walkthrough
XOR acts as a programmable inverter: when $b=0$, $out = a$; when $b=1$, $out = \sim a$. It forms the core arithmetic building block of half adders, full adders, parity generators, LFSRs, and cryptographic ciphers.

Silicon & Hardware Engineering Notes
🛠️ ASIC vs FPGA Hardware Considerations
Transmission gate XOR cells require only 4 or 6 transistors in custom CMOS libraries, making them highly area-efficient compared to building from separate NAND gates.
